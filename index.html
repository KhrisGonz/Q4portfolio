What is Ethical Hacking?

Ethical hacking is the practice of testing systems, networks, or applications for security weaknesses with permission. The goal is to find problems before malicious attackers do.

Ethical vs Illegal Hacking

The tools are often the same, but the key difference is authorization. Ethical hackers have permission and work to protect systems, while illegal hackers act without consent.

Why It Matters

Organizations rely on ethical hackers to identify vulnerabilities early. Without testing, these weaknesses could be exploited in real attacks.
